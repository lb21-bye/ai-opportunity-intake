AI Opportunity Intake for R&D

A single front door for AI ideas across R&D. Teams describe a repetitive task; AI recommends the right fix (prompt, automation, agent or app), sizes the time saved, and prioritises it in a shared backlog. The result: every idea in one place, quick wins shipped first, and nothing built twice.

Prototype brief

v0.1Concept, ready for pilotProblem, hypothesis, success measures and the build
Problem
AI ideas across R&D arrive through scattered Slack messages and meetings. There's no shared view of what exists, what's worth building, or what's risky.
Hypothesis
One structured front door, with AI triage, will surface the highest-value opportunities faster and stop teams duplicating tools. Routing low-effort fixes to trained citizen builders will get them shipped in days, not weeks.
What I built
A 7-question intake, automated enrichment from the directory, Claude triage (fix, hours, risk), and a ranked backlog with Slack hand-off.
Success measures
  • Idea to triage in under 24 hours
  • 80% of R&D teams submitting in month 1
  • Zero duplicate builds
  • Hours saved tracked per shipped fix
  • A citizen builder in every R&D team, shipping low-effort fixes
The build
AI-powered, fully automated
  1. 1Ask7 quick questions, mostly one tap. Logic jumps only when needed.
  2. 2AutomateTeam size and sponsor pulled from the directory.
  3. 3AI triageClaude recommends the fix, sizes hours saved, flags risk.
  4. 4Hand offSlack updates and a ranked backlog row. Zero manual admin.
