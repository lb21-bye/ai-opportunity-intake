AI Opportunity Intake for R&D

Any team describes a repetitive task in a short form. AI suggests the simplest fix (a prompt, an automation, an agent or an internal app), estimates the hours it would save, and adds it to a ranked backlog. Try it with a task of your own.

1. The intake form

7 quick tap-or-type questions and 1 optional one, then a thank-you screen. Follow-ups only appear through logic jumps, for example when personal data is involved.

2. What happens when you submit

1

Typeform

Collects the answers in a conversational form any R&D team can fill in.

2

Zapier

Typeform's native integration picks up each response and looks up team size from the company directory, so nobody has to count heads.

3

Glean

Searches for an existing tool or workflow first, so teams reuse before anyone rebuilds.

4

Claude

Chooses the simplest fix and estimates hours saved a year, with the Glean results as context.

5

Slack + backlog

Posts the recommendation to the team channel, updates the sponsor and their team lead, and adds a ranked row to the AI backlog.

3. The ranked AI backlog

#TaskTeamSuggested fixEffortRiskHours saved / yr

Example rows are illustrative. Ranking: hours saved a year, highest first.

How I'd run this in R&D

W1

Open the intake

Share the form with every R&D team and sit in on a few workflows to get past the first request to the real job to be done.

W2

Triage the backlog

Check Glean for anything that already exists, rank the rest by hours saved and effort, agree an owner for the top ten, and check access and data needs with IT and InfoSec early.

W4

Ship quick wins

Build the low-effort items first, define what good looks like before release, then track usage and feedback after launch.

W6

Report value

Show leaders hours saved, adoption and spend, and feed what was learned back into the next round of the backlog.

This is the same approach I used at Sage, where six agents and automations went live and saved 1,000+ hours a year.
